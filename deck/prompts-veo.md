# XPuesto · Prompts para Veo (en Gemini)

Dos videos. Cada uno lleva su imagen de referencia (primer cuadro) y su prompt. Los corres tú en Gemini con Veo, pegando el prompt y subiendo la referencia. Si el resultado no respeta la app, vuelve a correr con la misma referencia y agrega al final: "keep the phone screen exactly as in the reference image".

---

## Video 1 · «Tu puesto. Tu destino.» (30 s, vertical 9:16, para WhatsApp e Instagram)

**Imagen de referencia:** `deck/img/portada.jpg` (la pasajera en la parada virtual con el Corolla azul) + `deck/img/salidas.jpg` (la pantalla de salidas).

**Prompt (pegar en inglés):**

Cinematic vertical 9:16 video, 30 seconds, warm early-morning light in Caracas, Venezuela, Ávila mountains in the background. Use the reference photo as the first frame: a young Venezuelan woman with a navy carry-on suitcase at a modern shopping-mall "virtual stop", looking at her phone. One slow camera movement only: a gentle push-in that ends on the phone screen, which shows exactly the XPuesto app screen from the second reference image (white app, navy text, orange "Apartar mi puesto" button, list of departures Caracas → Valencia with driver photos and prices). Cut to a blue Toyota Corolla sedan pulling up, the driver in a light blue shirt gets out, greets her, photographs her suitcase with his phone and closes the trunk. Cut to the car on the highway to Valencia, mountains and green hills, the phone on the dashboard showing a small car icon moving along a route. Final frame: the woman arriving in Valencia, smiling, and the XPuesto logo (orange X with road stripes, navy "puesto" wordmark) over white with the tagline "Tu puesto. Tu destino."

Sound: soft acoustic guitar with a subtle Venezuelan cuatro feel, street ambience, a car door closing, the highway hum. Voice-over in warm Venezuelan Spanish, male, calm and confident:
"¿Vas pa' Valencia? Aparta tu puesto desde el teléfono. Chofer verificado, tu maleta con foto, y una parada segura. Sin terminal, sin espera. XPuesto: tu puesto, tu destino."

On-screen text, white bold sans-serif, one line at a time, in Spanish: "Aparta tu puesto" · "Equipaje con foto" · "Parada segura" · final: "XPuesto · xpuesto.app". No other text. No extra logos. Natural skin, real Venezuelan people, no exaggerated smiles.

## Video 2 · «Sale lleno, sale pago» (15 s, vertical 9:16, para captar choferes)

**Imagen de referencia:** `deck/img/chofer-bolsa.jpg` (la bolsa de trabajo) + una foto de `public/chofer-jose.jpg` (el chofer).

**Prompt (pegar en inglés):**

Vertical 9:16 video, 15 seconds, documentary style, mid-morning light at a bus terminal parking area in Caracas, Venezuela. A Venezuelan driver around 40, short dark hair, light blue shirt (match the reference portrait), leans on his blue Toyota Corolla looking at his phone. One camera movement only: a slow orbit from his shoulder to the phone screen, which shows exactly the XPuesto driver app from the reference image (white app, cards "Rutas llenas sin chofer", orange button "Tomar esta ruta"). He taps the button, nods, gets in the car. Cut: four passengers boarding at a mall entrance, trunk closing. Cut: the car on the highway. Final frame: the phone showing "A tu billetera $40,80" over white, then the XPuesto logo (orange X, navy "puesto").

Sound: engine start, doors, upbeat but calm percussion with a hint of Venezuelan cuatro. Voice-over in Venezuelan Spanish, male, direct, like a friend talking to a driver:
"Deja de esperar en el terminal. Con XPuesto tomas rutas que ya van llenas y pagas. Sales, llegas, cobras en tu billetera. Regístrate como chofer."

On-screen text in Spanish, one line at a time: "Rutas llenas" · "Sales pago" · "Cobras al llegar" · final: "Regístrate en xpuesto.app". No other text, no extra logos.
